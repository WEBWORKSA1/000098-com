Accessibility statement

Last updated October 27, 2026

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA across the site. The site supports keyboard navigation with visible focus, respects reduced-motion preferences, uses semantic headings and labels for every form field, and keeps text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher.

Known limitations

Some color swatches are, by nature, low contrast against the page; their values are always provided as text. Embedded third-party videos may not meet every criterion.

Feedback

If something is hard to use, tell us through the contact page. We aim to respond within five business days.
