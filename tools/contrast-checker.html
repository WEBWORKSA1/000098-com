How to use the contrast checker

What ratio do I need?

4.5:1 for normal text (AA), 7:1 for AAA, and 3:1 for large text and interface components such as icons and focus rings.

How is the fix suggestion found?

We keep the hue and saturation of your text color and step its lightness until the pair reaches 4.5:1.
