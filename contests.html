Rules

  1. Five colors, one of which must be #000098.
  2. Every text pair you propose must reach WCAG AA (4.5:1). Check with the contrast checker.
  3. Give the palette a name and a one-sentence use case.
  4. Entries must be your own work. Palettes copied from other sites will be disqualified.
  5. Open to anyone 18 or older, where permitted by local law. No purchase necessary.
  6. By entering you allow 000098 to display your palette with credit. You keep your rights.
  7. Judging criteria: harmony (40%), accessibility (30%), originality (20%), usefulness (10%).
