Privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information 000098.com ("we") collects and how we use it. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (quote, contact, pledge, contest entry, sponsorship or job application) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. Forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes the submission on our behalf. We use this information only to respond to you and to run the service you asked about. We do not sell it.

Tools

Our color tools run in your browser. Images you open in the image color picker are not uploaded to us.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. Choosing "Essential only" requests non-personalized ads.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. YouTube's own privacy policy applies once a video loads.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Quebec Law 25, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to processing. Contact us through the contact page and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and contests are open only to adults.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
