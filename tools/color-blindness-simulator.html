How to use the color blindness simulator

How accurate is this?

It uses the Machado et al. (2009) model at full severity, with partial severity for the -anomaly types. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

What should I change if colors look the same?

Increase lightness differences, add labels or patterns, and avoid relying on red versus green.
